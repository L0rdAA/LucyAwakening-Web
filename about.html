About Lucy’s Awakening

Lucy’s Awakening is the second adventure in Shade – The Dark Knight, a fantasy role-playing series. Lucy learned swordsmanship to fulfil her father’s wishes. When a noise wakes her at camp, she sets out to investigate.

Meet the characters

  • Lucy: A swordswoman training near Terra.
  • Lus: Lucy’s older brother and an experienced fighter.
  • Ashely: An archer who dreams of a world free of evil.
  • Mike: A bounty hunter and explorer.
  • Cecilia: An apprentice witch.
