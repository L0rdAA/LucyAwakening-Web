Story and project

Lucy's Awakening is a browser role-playing game in the world of Shade – The Dark Knight. Lucy trained in swordsmanship to fulfil her father's wishes; at the opening camp she hears a disturbance and investigates.

Meet a few characters

Lus is Lucy's older brother and an experienced fighter. Ashely is an archer who dreams of a world free of evil. Mike is a bounty hunter and explorer, while Cecilia is an apprentice of witchcraft. These introductions come from the game's character profiles; they do not imply when a character joins.
