Privacy

Updated 20 November 2026

Lucy’s Awakening is operated by Shade – The Dark Knight. For privacy questions, use the contact details on About.

Game saves and settings

The game stores saves and settings locally in your browser. There is no player account or cloud save sync. Use the same browser profile and site address to keep your progress. Clearing this site’s browser data can permanently remove saves and settings.

Hosting

Netlify hosts the site and game files. Hosting and security services process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. See Netlify’s privacy information.

Optional advertising

Play is ad-free by default. On supported desktop screens, you can choose to show one Adsterra banner beside the game on the same Play page. Mobile stays ad-free. The choice is not saved for future visits. Save in the game menu before enabling or turning off ads, since Play may reload.

When enabled, the third-party ad code runs with this site’s browser permissions and could read or change locally stored game saves and settings. Play uses a short-lived first-party cookie only to turn the banner on for that load. Adsterra and its partners may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser information, or use cookies and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads. See Adsterra’s privacy policy.

Turning off the banner does not remove information that may already have been stored. The AdSense site verification tag does not by itself request ads.

External links and email

The trilogy site and social links lead to separate services with their own privacy practices. If you email the project, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include so we can reply. Please don’t send passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Your choices

Your browser settings let you manage stored site data. Clearing it can delete progress. Contact the project with questions about information you’ve sent.
