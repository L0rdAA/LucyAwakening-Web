Privacy

Updated 21 October 2026

Lucy’s Awakening is operated by Shade – The Dark Knight. For privacy questions, contact ShadeTheDarkKnightGame@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

The game stores progress and settings in your browser’s local storage on this site. It does not offer cloud save synchronization or require a player account. Continue using the same browser and site address to access your progress. Clearing this site’s browser data can permanently remove your saves and settings.

Hosting and technical information

Netlify hosts the site and delivers the game files. Hosting and security services process technical information associated with requests, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. See Netlify’s privacy information for more about the provider.

Advertising

Advertising is not currently enabled. The site contains a Google AdSense ownership-verification meta tag, which does not itself load advertising scripts or request ads. We will update this notice and the applicable privacy choices before enabling advertising.

External links and contact

The story website and social links lead to separate services with their own privacy practices. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Your choices

You can manage this site’s stored data in your browser settings, but doing so may delete game progress. Contact us with questions about information you have sent us. This notice describes the current game site; it will be updated when its practices change.
