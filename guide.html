Player guide

Learn the controls, save your game, or find your next objective. The walkthrough covers the opening route and avoids ending spoilers.

Controls

Click or tap inside the game first.

  • Move: Arrow keys, D-pad, or click or tap the destination.
  • Confirm: Enter, Z, Space, left click, or tap.
  • Back or menu: Esc, X, right click, or two-finger tap. Outside dialogue, this opens the menu.

Save and return

Open the game menu, choose Save, select a slot and confirm. Use Continue on the title screen next time. Keep a second slot before a difficult stretch if you want an earlier point to return to.

Save before reloading or leaving Play. Your slots are stored in this browser profile at this site address, not in an account. Clearing site data can delete them.

Lucy’s camp

  1. Read the tutorial board for equipment, healing, battle rewards and saving.
  2. Open both chests for the Practice Sword, Barbarian Cloth and 15 gold. Open Equip to put the items on, or choose Optimize.
  3. Investigate the noise. Follow the story objective rather than the road to NewStar or the Honey Farm sign.

Terra’s Greenlands

  1. Help the soldiers defeat 15 Orcs. Check the remaining count, not the number of battles: some fights have multiple Orcs.
  2. If Lucy is low on health, rest at her tent or look for Potion Vials in Greenlands chests.
  3. Speak to the stationed Terra Soldier to turn in the quest for 50 EXP and 150 gold. If he sends you back, revisit soldiers or paths you missed.
  4. Head south to the Orc cave and help Terra’s Commander. Back in Terra’s Village, pick up his Private Training Note for NewStar.

Terra’s Village side quests

No Longer Buzzing!

Speak to the trader, then go southeast to Honey Farm. Defeat six Hornets and collect three intact Honey Combs; crushed combs do not count. The trader gives two Honey, 180 EXP and 270 gold, and starts selling Honey.

A Lass Request

Bring the villager five Cockatrice Feathers and five White Cockatrice Feathers. Check each count separately.

Quest Log

The Quest Log shows active, completed and failed quests. If you’re stuck, check the goal, remaining counts, or whether you still need to speak to a quest giver. After the Commander introduces quests, look for the NEW marker. You can reread notes and letters from your inventory.

NewStar and Selpa

  • Knighthood Test: Get the Wolves Den basement key from the NewStar examiner. Clothing Industry 1 is a separate trader quest for four Fox Hides in the same area.
  • River Rafting: Collect seven Wood Logs and two Wood Planks to help build the raft to Selpa.
  • Bronze Mine: Clear monsters in the mine west of Selpa for the mayor. The trader’s separate request for ten Vicious Cotton can be done on the same trip.

Troubleshooting

  • Controls don’t respond: Click or tap inside the game and close any open dialogue.
  • No sound: Check that the tab and device aren’t muted, then check Options in the game.
  • Missing saves: Return to the original browser profile and address. Don’t clear site data or overwrite a slot while checking.
  • Game won’t load: Give it time, then note any error message. For bug reports, use the contact details on About.
