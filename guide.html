Player guide

If a game is running in another tab, leave it open. Navigating away from the game does not save your progress.

Controls

Click or tap inside the game first. Use the arrow keys, D-pad, or a click or tap on a destination to move. Enter, Z or Space confirms; Esc or X cancels and opens the menu. Right click or a two-finger touch also goes back.

First steps at camp

  1. Read the tutorial board for movement, items, equipment, monsters and saving.
  2. Open the chest for a Practice Sword and Barbarian Cloth. Open Equip to put them on; collecting gear does not equip it automatically.
  3. Follow the opening objective. Some routes are blocked until you progress; read Lucy's warnings and signs, including the warning about hornets near Honey Farm.

Quests and preparation

Open Quest Log from the game menu to inspect active objectives, including main and side quests. The opening objectives concern the Orc attack near Terra. Check item counts before returning to a quest giver. Review equipment, HP and MP before exploring further.

Save and return

When the menu is available, open it, choose Save, select a slot and confirm. Wait until saving finishes before closing the tab. Choose Continue on the title screen to load a saved slot. Consider using more than one slot before difficult sections.

Saves are local to the browser profile and site address; they are not synced to an account. Another browser, device, private session or preview site may not have them. Clearing site data can delete progress. See the privacy notice.

Troubleshooting

Controls do not respond

Click inside the game and try again. Finish any open dialogue before moving; try touch or mouse to distinguish a focus issue.

No sound

Click inside the game, check the tab and device volume, and review the game's Options volume controls.

Loading or missing saves

Allow time for loading and check the connection. Record any error message. Save before reloading if possible. For a missing save, check the original browser profile and site address; do not clear site data or overwrite another slot while investigating. Contact the project with the location, browser and steps.
