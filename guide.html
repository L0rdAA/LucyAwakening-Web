Player guide

Get out of camp, finish Terra's first two story objectives, and decide which optional jobs to take before leaving town. This guide reveals opening objectives, not the end of the story.

If the game is running in another tab, leave it open. Navigating away from the game does not save your progress.

Controls

Click or tap inside the game first. Move with the arrow keys, D-pad, or by selecting a destination with a mouse or touch. Enter, Z or Space confirms. Esc or X cancels and opens the menu outside dialogue. Right click or a two-finger touch also goes back.

Get ready at Lucy's camp

  1. Read the tutorial board. It covers movement, equipment, recovery items, battle rewards and saving.
  2. Search the chests. One holds a Practice Sword and Barbarian Cloth; the other holds 15 gold. Collecting equipment does not equip it. In the Equip menu, choose the items yourself or use the game's Optimize option to select from what you own.
  3. Investigate the noise. Follow Lucy's opening objective rather than the road to NewStar. If Lucy warns that a route is too dangerous, return to the story route. The Honey Farm sign is a warning, not a requirement to go there now.

Terra's Greenlands: from the first Orc to the commander

  1. The first encounter sets up the story quest to defeat 15 Orcs. Follow the field encounters and help the Terra soldiers who are still fighting. Use the remaining count, rather than the number of fights, to check your progress: some events pit Lucy against more than one Orc.
  2. If Lucy takes too much damage, return to the tent for a nap. Look for chests in the Greenlands for Potion Vials and check your equipment again if a battle is harder than expected.
  3. Once all the attacking Orcs are defeated, speak to the stationed Terra Soldier. If he still asks you to finish the fight, check the remaining count and revisit soldiers or paths you passed earlier. Turning in the completed quest awards 50 EXP and 150 gold.
  4. The soldier directs you south along the route toward the Orc cave. Find and assist Terra's Commander there. Back in Terra's Village, the Commander gives Lucy a Private Training Note for advanced sword training in NewStar.

Two optional jobs in Terra's Village

No Longer Buzzing!

Speak with the trader before going to Honey Farm, southeast of the village. Defeat six Hornets and collect three Honey Combs. Some combs you inspect are crushed and do not count; keep looking until the Quest Log shows three. Returning to the trader awards two Honey, 180 EXP and 270 gold, and makes Honey available from the trader.

A Lass Request

A villager is looking for five Cockatrice Feathers and five White Cockatrice Feathers. These are separate objectives, so a surplus of one kind does not finish the other. Check both counts before going back to the requester.

When to use the Quest Log

The menu's Quest Log separates active, completed and failed quests. A story quest advances the main route, while optional jobs can stay active alongside it. For a stalled quest, first read the objective, then check whether a count is missing or whether you still need to talk to the person who sent you.

After the Commander introduces quests in Terra, look for the NEW marker for a story objective that needs a conversation. Notes and letters can be checked again from the inventory when you need a hint.

NewStar and the route to Selpa

  • Knighthood Test: The NewStar examiner gives Lucy a key to the basement of Wolves Den for the Courage Test. Do not confuse this story quest with the optional NewStar trader job, Clothing Industry 1, which asks for four Fox Hides from Wolves Den.
  • River Rafting: To help the stranded man build the raft to Selpa, collect seven Wood Logs and two Wood Planks. The Quest Log tracks the supplies separately.
  • Selpa's Bronze Mine: The mayor wants the monsters cleared from the mine west of town. A separate trader request sends you to the same mine for ten Vicious Cotton. Accept both if you want to track both objectives on the same trip.

Save and return

Open the in-game menu, choose Save, select a slot and confirm. Keep a separate slot before a difficult stretch if you want the option to return. Use Continue on the title screen to load an existing slot.

Saves belong to the browser profile and site address, not an account. Another browser, device, private session or preview site may not have them. Clearing site data can delete progress. See the privacy notice.

Troubleshooting

Controls do not respond

Click or tap inside the game, then try again. Finish or close open dialogue before moving. Test a click or tap if the keyboard still does not respond.

No sound

Start by clicking inside the game. Check whether the tab or device is muted, then review the volume controls in the game's Options menu.

Loading or missing saves

Allow time for loading and record any error message. Save before reloading if you can. For a missing save, first check the original browser profile and address. Do not clear site data or overwrite another slot while investigating. Contact the project with the location, browser and steps.
