Lucy's Awakening

Play as Lucy, a swordswoman trained to fulfil her father's wishes. Her adventure begins at a camp near Terra, where a disturbance interrupts her rest.

Move: arrow keys, D-pad, touch or left click
Confirm: Enter, touch or left click · Menu / back: Esc, two-finger touch or right click

Save before leaving. Navigating away in this tab ends the running game and does not save it. Open the guide in a new tab to keep playing.

Begin at camp

Read the tutorial board and check the chest for a Practice Sword and Barbarian Cloth. Open Equip to put them on. The player guide (new tab) explains controls, quests and saving.

Returning to your adventure

Choose Continue on the title screen to load a saved slot. Saves stay in the same browser and site address; clearing site data can remove them.
