Lucy's Awakening

Lucy Wseley is training near Terra when the sound of trouble wakes her. What starts as a night at camp turns into a defense of the village and a journey across Galasca. This is the second adventure in Shade – The Dark Knight, a browser-playable fantasy role-playing game.

Jump to the game

Your first objective: defend Terra

Start at Lucy's camp, where the tutorial board explains the controls. Take the Practice Sword and Barbarian Cloth from the camp chest, then open Equip to put them on. Lucy's tent offers a quick nap; it is a useful place to return to if you are injured during the opening fights.

In Terra's Greenlands, help the soldiers in the field and track the first story quest's 15 Orcs. After the count reaches zero, speak to the soldier stationed on the route. He points Lucy south toward the Orc cave to find Terra's Commander. The opening walkthrough (new tab) explains what to check if the objective stalls.

Optional jobs can wait until Terra is safe. One of the village traders sends Lucy to Honey Farm to fight six Hornets and bring back three intact Honey Combs. The Terra side-quest checklist (new tab) also covers the two kinds of Cockatrice feathers a villager asks for.

Move: arrow keys, D-pad, touch or left click
Confirm: Enter, touch or left click · Menu / back: Esc, two-finger touch or right click

Returning to your adventure

Save before leaving. The game does not save just because you close the tab. Use the in-game menu to save, then choose Continue on the title screen when you return. Use the same browser profile and site address; clearing site data can remove your saves.
